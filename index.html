Why hosts use it

Answer the 11pm questions before they get asked

Every guidebook is built from small blocks you can fill in, reorder in your own words, or leave out entirely.

Swap the pictures

Paste an image URL or upload from your phone. The first photo becomes the hero your guest sees.

Wi‑Fi without the squint

Network and password get a copy button, so nobody retypes a 16-character password twice.

Directions that work

Search an address, drop the pin, and choose whether the map opens the address or the exact coordinates.

Dark mode included

The guide follows your guest's phone setting, and they can flip it themselves from the hero photo.

One template, many places

Keep a guidebook per property. Duplicate a template and change only what differs between them.

Yours to keep

Download plain HTML. Email it, AirDrop it, print it, or host it anywhere. Nothing phones home.

How it works

Three steps, one file

The builder is password-protected for hosts. Drafts stay in this browser until you download them. Guests never create an account.

Pick a starting point

Open a template close to your place, or start from a blank guidebook.

Change what matters

Photos, door code, Wi‑Fi, parking, house rules, and a few honest local picks.

Download and send

Hit Download HTML. Send the file, or drop it on any host and share the link.
